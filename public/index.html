THE JOURNEY

From a simple idea
to a growing community.

2024

ORIGIN was born.

A new chapter began for the CSE Department's technical community. ORIGIN set out to create opportunities for students to explore technology, collaborate, and learn beyond the classroom.

2025

Our First Hackathon.

ORIGIN organized its first hackathon, bringing students together to turn ideas into practical solutions. It marked an important step in the club's journey toward hands-on technical learning and collaboration.

2025

A Growing Community.

ORIGIN began building its presence beyond campus activities, gaining college-level recognition and starting to establish its identity on social media. The focus was on connecting students with technical events, learning opportunities, and the wider community.

2026

AI Division Started

ORIGIN is exploring the world of Artificial Intelligence, encouraging students to learn emerging technologies, experiment with ideas, and build practical skills.

2026

Building Startups

From identifying real-world problems to developing project ideas, ORIGIN encourages students to think beyond the classroom and explore the possibilities of innovation and entrepreneurship.
